# Privacy Policy for Coursera Skip Video & Reading

**Last updated:** September 1, 2026

This extension is an open-source educational tool. We value your privacy and are committed to transparency.

## 1. Data Collection
This extension **does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal information, browsing history, cookies, credentials, or analytics.

## 2. Storage & Permissions
- `storage`: Used strictly to save user interface preferences locally on your device (such as language selection EN/VI).
- `activeTab` and `tabs`: Used solely to detect if you are currently viewing a Coursera lesson page when you click the extension.
- Host permissions (`https://www.coursera.org/*`): Used exclusively to interact with lesson progress endpoints on Coursera.org on your behalf.

## 3. Third Parties
We do not sell, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties. There are no tracking scripts, ads, or third-party analytical tools embedded.

## 4. Open Source
The entire source code is transparent and publicly auditable on GitHub:  
https://github.com/tuankiet18-dev/coursera-skip-tool
