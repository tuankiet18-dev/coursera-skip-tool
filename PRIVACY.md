# Privacy Policy for Coursera Skip Video & Reading

**Last updated:** September 20, 2026

This extension is an independent, open-source educational and productivity tool. We value your privacy and are committed to complete transparency.

---

## 1. Data Collection & Telemetry
This extension **does NOT collect, store, track, transmit, or share** any personal information, browsing history, account credentials, cookies, or telemetry analytics. There are no remote servers, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics scripts embedded.

## 2. Permissions & Data Usage
- `storage`: Used strictly to save user interface preferences locally on your device (such as language selection EN/VI) and your optional Gemini API key.
- `activeTab` and `tabs`: Used solely to detect if you are currently viewing an active Coursera lesson page when you open the extension or interact with the floating widget.
- Host permissions (`https://www.coursera.org/*`): Used exclusively to interact directly with lesson progress endpoints on Coursera on your behalf using your existing active browser session. No session cookies (such as `CAUTH`) are ever exfiltrated.
- Host permissions (`https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/*`, `https://api.openai.com/*`, `https://api.groq.com/*`): Used exclusively to communicate directly with official AI provider endpoints (Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Groq) if you provide your personal API key. Your key and prompts are transmitted directly from your browser to the official provider APIs and are never accessed, saved, or logged by the extension developer.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer any user data to any third party. The only external API communication possible is directly between the user's browser and official AI provider endpoints (Google AI Studio, OpenAI, Groq) if the user configures an AI key.

The popup includes an optional "Support" link that opens a donation page (hosted on GitHub Pages) in a new browser tab. The extension itself sends no data when you click it, and no feature is locked behind donations. Any payment is handled entirely by the third-party donation provider on its own page, under its own privacy policy.
